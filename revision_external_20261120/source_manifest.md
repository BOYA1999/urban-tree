# Canopy reference sources and study footprints

Metadata and downloads checked 20 November 2026. Dates below describe observations rather than release dates. The 2 m grid describes the common sampling used for this analysis.

| Reference | Observations and native representation | Domain and processing | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| London | 2020–2022 aerial RGB imagery at 25 cm, colour infrared at 50 cm and elevation layers. The 2024 release contains 467 canopy polygon tiles. Imagery and elevation inputs for 87% of tiles date to 2022. | Survey boundary from the union of Greater London boroughs excluding mean high water. Burn the original canopy polygons to the 2 m grid. | [GLA canopy polygons and methodology](https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/tree-canopy-cover-2024-2w4m3), [statistical GIS boundaries](https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/statistical-gis-boundary-files-for-london-20od9) |
| Paris | Summer 2021 IGN aerial imagery and elevation data. Published vegetation-height raster nominally 0.5 m, with numerical values returned by the service on request at 2 m. | Official Métropole du Grand Paris contour and served raster extent. Decode the public-domain offset of 100 and the outside-MGP offset of 1,000 before applying the provider's strict >3 m cutoff. | [Apur height product](https://www.apur.org/en/open-data-maps/open-data/height-vegetation-greater-paris-raster-data), [numeric ImageServer](https://carto2.apur.org/apur/rest/services/OPENDATA/HAUTEUR_VEGETATION_2021/ImageServer), [metropolitan contour](https://geo.api.gouv.fr/epcis/200054781?geometry=contour&format=geojson) |
| Melbourne | City of Melbourne 2021 high-resolution multispectral canopy polygons, 57,980 records. | City of Melbourne municipality. Burn the original canopy polygons to the 2 m grid. | [Canopy data](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/tree-canopies-2021-urban-forest/export/), [municipal boundary](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/municipal-boundary/export/) |
| São Paulo | May–July 2017 municipal airborne LiDAR, 0.5 m vegetation-height model, version 4. | 6 km radius from the GHSL centre coordinate intersected with the municipal boundary and surveyed vegetation-height mosaics. Establish validity with the official LiDAR tile geometries and each component raster's footprint in the mosaic. Height >3 m is canopy. | [Gomes height model](https://doi.org/10.34740/kaggle/dsv/4044806), [PMSP survey and acquisition index](https://doi.org/10.5069/G9NV9GD1), [IBGE municipal boundary](https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v3/malhas/municipios/3550308?formato=application%2Fvnd.geo%2Bjson&qualidade=maxima) |
| New York City | 2017 and 2021 land-cover rasters using local aerial observations and elevation data. | Shared footprint of both epochs. Both maps were sampled through their approximately 1.22 m overviews. | [2017 raster](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/he6d-2qns), [2021 raster](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14053441) |
| Toronto | 2018 forest and land-cover polygons mapped from high-resolution imagery with LiDAR support. | Municipal survey footprint, rasterized at 2 m. | [Toronto Forest and Land Cover](https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/forest-and-land-cover/) |

The London Datastore marks the canopy layer All Rights Reserved and lists the borough boundary data under Open Government Licence v2. The boundary attribution is “Contains National Statistics data © Crown copyright and database right [2015]” and “Contains Ordnance Survey data © Crown copyright and database right [2015]”. Apur lists the vegetation-height data under ODbL. The City of Melbourne portals list CC BY 4.0 for the canopy and municipal boundary. Kaggle lists the São Paulo vegetation-height data under CC BY-SA 4.0. OpenTopography specifies GPLv3 for the source São Paulo LiDAR. Retrieve the source data from their providers under the applicable terms.

The London, Melbourne, Paris-boundary and São Paulo downloads are enumerated in `code/fetch_reference_sources.py`. The Paris numerical requests and class decoding are in `code/prepare_external.py`. The original São Paulo window uses ten mosaics. The expanded São Paulo script selects mosaics from the municipal footprint and survey index. Expanded Melbourne uses the nominal 2022 band of the 5 m fractional tree raster and the companion catchment raster.
